## Champion Is a Johnson Controls Brand

Here's the key fact most homeowners don't know: Champion heating and cooling systems are manufactured by [Johnson Controls International](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johnson_Controls) (JCI), the same company that makes York, Coleman, and Luxaire. These aren't just "sister brands" — they're built in the same factories, by the same engineering teams, using the same quality control processes.

When you buy a Champion air conditioner or furnace, you're getting Johnson Controls engineering. The same compressors, the same coils, the same manufacturing standards. The primary differences between Champion and York are branding, distribution, and pricing — not quality.

This matters because Johnson Controls is not a small player. They're one of North America's largest HVAC manufacturers with decades of proven reliability. Champion gives homeowners access to that manufacturing quality at a more accessible price point.

> **Key Takeaway:** Champion HVAC equipment is manufactured by Johnson Controls — the same company behind York, Coleman, and Luxaire. Same factories, same engineering, same quality standards.
